## Conclusion and conditions

Our reading is that a free, non-commercial maintenance fork is within the FSL's
permitted purposes, provided the redistribution and other conditions are met.
This is a reading of the published terms, not a legal opinion or permission from
Kyle-Ye. Attribution alone would not cure an otherwise prohibited use.

| Licence provision | What it means for this release |
| --- | --- |
| License Grant and Permitted Purpose | Modification and redistribution are allowed for permitted purposes. The restriction concerns competing commercial products or services. A free download is not automatically non-commercial in every business context. This fork is being offered as a non-commercial maintenance project. |
| Redistribution | Retain the original terms and copyright notices in source and distributed copies. LICENSE.txt is unchanged; notices are bundled inside and alongside the app. |
| Trademarks | Copyright permission does not grant use of upstream product names or marks as this fork's own branding. The public app uses the distinct name Finder Menu Tools and a new icon; MenuHelper and Kyle-Ye are identified as the origin. No endorsement is claimed. |
| Grant of Future License | The MIT grant takes effect separately for each version after two years. The March 2025 base is less than two years old at this review date. We therefore retain FSL-1.1-MIT and do not present the entire fork as currently MIT-licensed. Commit timestamps alone are not proof of first public availability. |
| Patents and Disclaimer | Their terms remain applicable and are reproduced in the licence. |
